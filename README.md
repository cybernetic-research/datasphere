# Datasphere

**Play: https://cybernetic-research.github.io/datasphere/**

A WebXR cyberspace game for VR headsets (made on a Quest) and desktop browsers, after *The Lawnmower Man* and *Johnny Mnemonic*.

Cross the battlefield and break into the enemy's satellite uplink bunker. Jack into its mainframe and turn the space lasers on themselves. Then run home before the bunker goes up.

- Nine chapters: the battlefield, the bunker, a long-distance call through cyberspace, the mainframe run, the pyramid, the firewall trap, the Polybius core, crushing the sysop's deck, and the escape.
- In the headset it's first person, with controllers or hand tracking, and snap or smooth turning.
- On a desktop it's third person, with WASD, the mouse and Space.

The whole game is one HTML file, with three.js and Web Audio. There's nothing to install.
